# Stripe Nonprofit Discount - When to Apply

## ⏳ Timeline Recommendation

**NOW (Before 501(c)(3)):**
- ✅ Continue using Stripe with standard rates (2.9% + $0.30)
- ✅ Accept donations as a regular business/ministry
- ✅ Keep all transaction records
- ❌ **Don't apply for nonprofit discount yet**

**AFTER 501(c)(3) Approval:**
- ✅ Apply for Stripe nonprofit discount
- ✅ Submit your IRS determination letter
- ✅ Get reduced rate (2.2% + $0.30)

---

## 📋 Why Wait?

**Required Documentation for Stripe Nonprofit Program:**
1. Valid 501(c)(3) determination letter from IRS
2. EIN (Employer Identification Number)
3. Proof of nonprofit status
4. Official nonprofit name matching IRS records

**If you apply too early:**
- Application will be rejected
- May cause confusion in your account
- Need to reapply later anyway

---

## 🎯 What You Should Do Now

**1. Keep Your Current Stripe Setup**
- It's already configured correctly for donations
- Standard business rates apply
- Full functionality available

**2. Track Application Status**
Know when to apply:
- 501(c)(3) applications typically take **2-6 months**
- Once approved, you'll receive determination letter
- Then apply to Stripe immediately

**3. Bookmark This Link** (for when you're ready):
- https://stripe.com/nonprofits
- Have your determination letter PDF ready
- Keep your EIN documentation accessible

## 💰 Cost Impact During Waiting Period

**Example: $5,000 in donations while waiting:**
- Standard fees: **$155** (2.9% + $0.30 per transaction)
- Nonprofit fees: **$120** (2.2% + $0.30 per transaction)
- **Difference: $35** spread over 3-6 months

**The fee difference is minimal during the waiting period.**

**Monthly breakdown at $1,000/month:**
- Standard: ~$31/month
- Nonprofit: ~$25/month
- Monthly savings after approval: ~$6

Focus on getting your 501(c)(3) approval first! The small fee difference shouldn't delay your donation acceptance.

## 📞 Stripe Nonprofit Application Process

### When You're Ready (Post-501(c)(3)):

**Step 1: Go to Application Page**
- Visit: https://stripe.com/nonprofits
- Click "Apply for nonprofit pricing"

**Step 2: Submit Documentation**
```
Required items:
✓ IRS 501(c)(3) determination letter (PDF)
✓ EIN (Employer Identification Number)
✓ Organization's legal name (matching IRS records)
✓ Website URL
✓ Brief description of mission
```

**Step 3: Wait for Approval**
- Typical approval time: 1-2 weeks
- Stripe will review documentation
- May request additional information
- Notification via email

**Step 4: Activate New Rates**
- Discount applies to future transactions
- No retroactive fee adjustments
- Updated pricing takes effect immediately

## 🚨 Important Legal Notes

### While Waiting for 501(c)(3):

**DO:**
- ✅ Call them "donations" or "contributions"
- ✅ Thank supporters for their "generous support"
- ✅ Mention you're "applying for 501(c)(3) status"
- ✅ Keep detailed records of all income

**DON'T:**
- ❌ Claim donations are "tax-deductible"
- ❌ Use EIN from another organization
- ❌ Claim nonprofit status before approval
- ❌ Promise tax benefits you can't deliver

### Sample Messaging (Pre-501(c)(3)):

```html
"Your generous support helps Shelter House Music serve the 
Eastern North Carolina Christian music community. We are currently 
in the process of obtaining 501(c)(3) status.

Note: Donations are not currently tax-deductible as we are 
pending 501(c)(3) approval."
```
